Chapter 03Design Journal

DESIGN JOURNAL

Entry 01 01 / 03

What makes exploration rewarding?

↓

Reward is not always a collectible or a new ability. Sometimes it is the feeling that a space trusted the player enough to let them notice something for themselves.

curiosity / agency

I look for the moment when a player stops following a path and starts forming a question. That shift is often more valuable than any reward screen.

Entry 02 02 / 03

How I iterate mechanics.

↓
Sketchidea
→
Playsignal
→
Refineintent
↓

Keep the question stable, change one variable, and let the player response decide what survives.

A prototype is useful when it gives the team something specific to disagree about. The next iteration should answer one question, not every question.

Entry 03 03 / 03

A mechanic that failed.

↓

What happenedThe system offered too many choices before the player understood why they mattered.

↓

What I learnedComplexity only feels deep after the first decision feels clear.

Failure became useful once I stopped treating it as lost work. It showed exactly where the player needed a stronger reason to care.
