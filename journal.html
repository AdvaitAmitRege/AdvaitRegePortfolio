Working notes / selected entries

Questions before answers.

A growing record of the observations, failures, and small decisions that shape how I design.

Entry 0101 / 07

What makes exploration rewarding?

Reward is not always a collectible or a new ability. Sometimes it is the feeling that a space trusted the player enough to let them notice something for themselves.

curiosity / agency
Entry 0202 / 07

How I iterate mechanics.

Keep the question stable, change one variable, and let the player response decide what survives.

iteration / playtest
Entry 0303 / 07

A mechanic that failed.

Complexity only feels deep after the first decision feels clear.

failure / learning
Entry 0404 / 07

Players rarely take the route I planned.

A level is a conversation, not a corridor. The most useful paths are often the ones players invent after the intended path stops making sense.

agency / level design
Entry 0505 / 07

Good friction has a shape.

Difficulty becomes interesting when the player can name the decision they are struggling with. Without that clarity, friction is only noise.

balance / clarity
Entry 0606 / 07

Production is part of authorship.

Every cut changes the experience. The producer's job is not to protect every idea, but to protect the idea that makes the project worth finishing.

scope / intent
Entry 0707 / 07

Make the next decision visible.

When players know what they can try next, uncertainty becomes curiosity. That small distinction changes the emotional temperature of a whole system.

ux / feedback
